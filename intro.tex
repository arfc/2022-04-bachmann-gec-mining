    \begin{columns}
        \column[t]{5cm}
    \begin{itemize}
    \item Multiple new reactor designs require \gls{HALEU} fuel, which allows for: 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Longer cycle times
        \item Higher burnups 
    \end{itemize}
    \item<1-> To meet the \gls{HALEU} demand, the U.S. \gls{DOE} has proposed two methods \cite{griffith_overview_2020}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Recovery and downblending of \gls{HEU}
        \item Enrichment of natural uranium
    \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Objectives}
    This work explores how developing a supply chain of \gls{HALEU} affects 
    the nuclear fuel cycle in the US. 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Quantify material requirements of the transition to reactors 
              fueled by \gls{HALEU}
        \pause
        \item Perform sensitivity analysis to understand how each of these metrics 
              are affected by model parameters
        \pause
        \item Identify potential fuel cycles that are optimized for specific objectives
        \pause 
        \item Investigate how the impurities in \gls{HEU} stockpiles affects reactor neutronics
    \end{itemize}
