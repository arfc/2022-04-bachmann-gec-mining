\begin{frame}
      \frametitle{Conclusions}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item The number of advanced reactors required scales with the 
              power output of the reactors. 
        \item Including the Xe-100 to the deployment scheme decreases 
              the mass of enriched uranium needed.
        \item The mass of feed uranium required is on a similar magnitud
              to what has historically been required by the \glspl{LWR} 
              unless only the \gls{MMR} is deployed. 
        \item Similar patterns are observed for the \gls{SWU} requirements. 
      \end{itemize}
      More detailed results for similar work can be found in \cite{bachmann_enrichment_2021}.
\end{frame}
